## 🚀 Overview

**MoneySence** is a next-gen personal finance application that removes the friction of manual expense tracking. By leveraging AI agents, automated statement parsing, and secure authentication, MoneySence empowers users to stay on top of their budgets, subscriptions, and holistic financial health in real time.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **🤖 AI Financial Coach (`/copilot`):** Conversational AI agent that delivers context-aware budgeting advice and proactive financial reviews.
*   **📥 Statement Ingestion (`/upload`):** Drag-and-drop support for bank statements and CSV/PDF parsing powered by automated OCR categorization.
*   **📊 Advanced Analytics (`/analytics`):** Deep-dive velocity metrics comparing monthly income versus expenses and weekday vs. weekend spending.
*   **❤️ Financial Health Audit (`/health`):** Holistic scoring evaluating your emergency liquidity buffer and debt-to-income stability.
*   **🎯 Budget Planner (`/budget`):** Category-specific spending caps and visual progress meters with a custom budget creation modal.
*   **🔔 Subscriptions & Bills (`/subscriptions`):** Centralized tracking for recurring monthly charges like streaming services and utility bills to avoid creep.
*   **⚙️ Settings & Customization (`/settings`):** Localization controls, custom notification toggles, and secure account configurations.
*   **🔐 Secure Authentication (`/login` & `/register`):** Modern OAuth-simulated login and account creation workflows.
